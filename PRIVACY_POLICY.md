# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 24, 2026**

## Introduction

Have You Seen This ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: When creating or managing an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and other profile information.
- **Content You Share**: Information, photos, links, and other content you create, upload, or share through the Service.
- **Shared Group Information**: Data related to groups you create or join, including group names, descriptions, and member lists.
- **Communications**: Messages, notes, and feedback you send to us.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- **Usage Information**: Features used, content accessed, interaction patterns, and time spent in the app.
- **Log Data**: Server logs containing IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.
- **Location Information**: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise GPS data unless you grant permission).

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Social Media**: If you link social media accounts, we receive information as authorized by those platforms.
- **Service Providers**: Analytics and crash reporting services may collect usage data.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

- **Service Provision**: Delivering, maintaining, and improving the Service
- **User Experience**: Personalizing content and features
- **Communication**: Sending service updates, support responses, and policy notifications
- **Analytics**: Understanding usage patterns and app performance
- **Security**: Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- **Compliance**: Meeting legal and regulatory requirements
- **Legal Claims**: Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### 3.1 We Do NOT Share Your Information With:

- Third-party advertisers (we do not display personalized ads)
- Data brokers or marketing firms
- Other users without your explicit consent

### 3.2 We MAY Share Your Information With:

- **Service Providers**: Cloud storage, analytics, and crash reporting services (under data processing agreements)
- **Legal Requirements**: Law enforcement, courts, or government agencies when legally required
- **Safety**: To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale

### 3.3 Public Information

Information you choose to share in public groups or shared content may be visible to other group members.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

### 4.1 Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: User data is stored locally on your device using encrypted databases
- **Cloud Backup**: Optional cloud backups are encrypted and stored securely
- **Retention**: We retain data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward

### 4.2 Security Measures

We implement:
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- Secure authentication protocols
- Regular security audits
- HTTPS encryption for data in transit
- Access controls and data minimization practices

**Note**: While we strive to protect your information, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- **Access**: Request what personal data we hold about you
- **Correction**: Update or correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Opt-Out**: Disable certain data collection features
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured format
- **Withdraw Consent**: Revoke previously granted permissions

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in Section 10.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13 (COPPA compliance). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data was collected, we will delete it immediately. Parents who believe their child has provided information should contact us.

## 7. Third-Party Services

### 7.1 Third-Party Links

Our app may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before use.

### 7.2 Analytics & Crash Reporting

- **Firebase Analytics**: Collects anonymous usage data (can be disabled in settings)
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Collects crash reports and error logs (can be disabled in settings)

Visit Google's Privacy Policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 8. Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions:

- **Camera & Gallery**: To upload and share photos
- **Storage**: To save and access local files
- **Contacts**: Optional, to help find and invite friends
- **Network**: To sync and share data with the cloud

You can manage these permissions in your device settings at any time.

## 9. Data Retention

- **Deleted Accounts**: We retain data for 30 days after deletion for recovery purposes, then permanently delete it
- **Backup Data**: Cloud backups are retained per your settings
- **Log Data**: Server logs are retained for up to 90 days
